About MusicBook

MusicBook is a free online music toolkit created for musicians, students and music lovers. Every tool runs directly in your browser — there is nothing to install and nothing to sign up for.


The current version (v1.1.0) includes an instrument tuner, a virtual tanpura, a metronome, an electronic tabla, a chord dictionary and a BPM finder. Future versions will add user accounts, saved projects and AI-powered audio tools like vocal separation and raga detection.
