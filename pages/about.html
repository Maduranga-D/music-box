About MusicBook

MusicBook is a free online music toolkit built for musicians, students and music lovers everywhere. Every tool runs directly in your browser — nothing to install, nothing to sign up for, and nothing standing between you and your practice session.

Why MusicBook

What Makes It Different

No app store, no account, no ads — just open a tool and start making music.

⚡

Instant, Zero Install

Every tool is a webpage. Open it and it works — no downloads, no updates, no waiting.

🌏

East Meets West

A tanpura drone and tabla taals sit right next to a guitar tuner and chord dictionary — built for every kind of musician.

🔒

Privacy First

Microphone audio for the tuner is processed entirely on your device. Nothing is recorded, uploaded or stored.

📱

Works Everywhere

Fully responsive layout, so the same tools work just as well on your phone as they do on a laptop.

🎁

Always Free

MusicBook is, and will always be, free to use. No premium tier, no paywalled tools.

💬

Built With Feedback

Roadmap

Where MusicBook Is Headed

Built one version at a time, in the open.

v1.1 · Current

The Complete Toolkit

Tuner, tanpura, metronome, tabla, chord dictionary, BPM finder and tuning reference — all built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, hosted on GitHub Pages.

v2.0 · Planned

Accounts & Saved Progress

A React-powered rebuild with user accounts, a Firebase backend, saved practice sessions and a community space for musicians to share tips.

v3.0 · Future

AI-Powered Tools

A Python backend adding vocal separation, automatic raga detection, vocal range analysis and automatic music notation transcription.
